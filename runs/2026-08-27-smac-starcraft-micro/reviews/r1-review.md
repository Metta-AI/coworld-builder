### B1 — `sim.battleIndex` is inside `gameHash` but is written only by the live server loop, outside `sim.step`; every multi-battle replay therefore diverges from its recorded hash chain one tick after the first battle ends
- Where: `src/smac/sim_state.nim:309`; `src/smac/server.nim:2087`, `src/smac/server.nim:2096-2106`; `src/smac/replays.nim:496-510`; `replay-viewer/smac_replay.nim:56-63`
- Observed, traced step by step:
  1. `src/smac/sim_state.nim:309` mixes `battleIndex` into the hash inside the micro block: `result.mixHashInt(sim.battleIndex)` (alongside `ourHp … battlesWon`).
  2. The only write in the entire tree is `src/smac/server.nim:2106` — `sim.battleIndex = gamesPlayed` — inside the live loop's `phaseBeforeStep != GameOver and sim.phase == GameOver` branch, i.e. **outside `sim.step`**. `grep -rn battleIndex src/ replay-viewer/ tests/` returns exactly three hits: the hash mix, that write, and `src/smac/sim.nim:4024` (`result.battleIndex = 0` in `initSimServer`).
  3. The hash for the battle-end tick T is written *before* that assignment (`server.nim:2087` `replayWriter.writeHash(uint32(sim.tickCount), sim.gameHash())`, then the `inc gamesPlayed` block at 2096), so tick T still matches. From tick **T+1** onward the live recording carries `battleIndex = 1` (then 2).
  4. On playback nothing sets it. `stepReplay` (`replays.nim:512-523`) calls `sim.step` then `checkReplayHash`; the only playback mirror of the battle switch is `advanceReplayGame` (`replays.nim:496-510`), which sets `gameHill`, `gameRegimes`, `gameIndex`, `regime` — **not `battleIndex`** (and its own docstring says those four are "written only by the live loop, and none of them is in `gameHash`", which is true of them and false of `battleIndex`). So the re-derived sim keeps `battleIndex = 0` forever.
  5. Consequence: `sim.gameHash()` at tick T+1 differs from the recorded hash by construction, for every episode with `maxGames > 1` — which is every shipped variant (`maxGames: 3`) and the certification fixture.
- CI evidence: the `wasm-viewer` job's *Native-to-wasm determinism gate* prints `Replay hash mismatch at tick 319; expected 2868868009740746775, got 2867911434624392430.` (run 33046300533, wasm-viewer step at 06:38:35Z). Tick 319 is consistent with battle 1 of the 480-tick fixture ending at tick 318. The three float removals the builder chased (`AGENTS.md` §OPEN) each *moved* the tick because each changed when battle 1 ends, not because any of them was the cause — **inferred** from the mechanism above.
- Spectator-visible evidence: the CI `viewer-smoke.png` (run 33046300533, `viewer-smoke` artifact) shows the shipped bundle rendering the banner **`REPLAY HASH MISMATCH — SHOWING RECORDED INPUTS`** across the top of the board.
- Checklist item: **2** — "Replaying the recorded events through the sim reproduces the recorded per-tick state **frame by frame**".
- Why blocking: the recorded hash chain — this repo's own definition of per-tick state, and the reason the enemy army can cost zero replay bytes — does not reproduce. Every hosted replay of a 3-battle episode shows the integrity warning, and after the first mismatch `checkReplayHash` sets `hashValidationFailed` and stops checking (`replays.nim:465-468`), so the chain is dead for the rest of the file. Note for severity calibration: `battleIndex` is read by nothing (three references total), so the *rendered* state after the divergence is otherwise identical — the damage is the integrity signal itself, not the picture.
