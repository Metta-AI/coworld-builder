### Rules and constants (the note's numbered order 1–8 / 6.1–6.11)

**N2 — the flow-field cell is 12 px, not the note's 34.** `sim_types.nim:827-839 HordeNavCell* = 12`, with an in-code rationale (34 px leaves no node inside a 26 px corridor; a zombie pressed west for 2000 ticks was measured). design.md:161-163 pins `NavCell` = 34 px. **N3 — a knight's `intercept` standoff is 44 px, not the note's 0.** `control.nim:358-373` returns `pointToward(leader, gate, knightReach - 8)` with a documented measurement (walking onto the leader crosses its own 26 px kill radius; a measured `phalanx`×4 episode ended `casualty` after two kills). design.md:664,668-670 says `roleStandoff` = 0 for a knight ("walk onto it").
