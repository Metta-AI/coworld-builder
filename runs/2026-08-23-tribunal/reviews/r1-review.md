### F1 — a `deadline` ending that trips at or after the ballot opens re-derives as `reason: "complete"` — **blocking-candidate (checklist item 2: "Replaying the recorded events through the sim reproduces the recorded per-tick state frame by frame")**

- Where: `src/tribunal/sim.nim:711-718` (`beginDeadlineBallot`), `src/tribunal/sim.nim:557-558` (`settle`), `src/tribunal/sim.nim:987-990` (`replayMatch`'s `evEnd` arm), `src/tribunal/server.nim:271-277` (the pre-turn deadline check)
- Observed (traced, then **executed**):
  - `replayMatch` recovers the deadline jump only from a vote event seen while the phase is still `phArgument`: `of evVote: sim.beginDeadlineBallot()` (sim.nim:978-982), and `beginDeadlineBallot` is `if sim.done or sim.phase != phArgument: return` (sim.nim:715-716).
  - If the argument rounds resolved normally, `resolveRound` → `openBallot` already set `phase = phBallot` (sim.nim:570-573, 301-305). A `forceBallot()` at that point (`sim.nim:720-737`) sets `sim.reason = "deadline"` live, but on replay `beginDeadlineBallot` is a no-op, the three recorded votes apply, the third one calls `settle()`, and `settle` does `if sim.reason.len == 0: sim.reason = "complete"` (sim.nim:557-558).
  - The recorded `end` event cannot repair it: `of evEnd: if not sim.done: …` (sim.nim:988) and the sim is already `done`.
  - Live path that produces it: the deadline is checked at the top of every turn, and the ballot **is** a turn (`server.nim:259-277`). A play budget exhausted during the closing argument round is therefore detected at the start of the ballot turn, with `phase == phBallot` — the most likely deadline shape, not a corner case.
- Executed (`nim r --path:src`, seeds 21/22/23, `rounds = 2`, one real juror vote then `forceBallot()`):
  ```
  seed 21 live.reason=deadline replay.reason=complete
          diffs=@["reason: live=\"deadline\" replay=\"complete\""]
          replayed own end event: complete   recorded end event: deadline
          results.reason live="deadline"     replay="complete"
  ```
  Exactly one field of `tableStateJson()` differs; the same divergence appears in the replayed sim's own `end` event text and in `resultsJson()`.
- Checklist item: 2 — the re-derived final frame is not the recorded final frame.
- Why blocking: `tools/ci/docker_smoke.sh` and every viewer path build their frames from `replayMatch` (`server.nim:148-152`, `replay-viewer/tribunal_replay.nim:36-37`). On a ballot-phase deadline the bundle's state timeline reports `reason: "complete"` while the replay's own `results` block says `"deadline"`. Blast radius is small — `stateToView` (renderer.js:1251-1272) does not copy `reason`, and the endscreen reads `payload.results.reason` (renderer.js:1160-1166, 1191), so nothing visibly wrong appears on screen today — but the frame-by-frame identity the checklist names is falsified, and the sim-level test that would catch it does not exist.
- Test coverage: `tests/test_sim.nim:383-394` exercises only the **mid-argument** deadline (`short.applyArgument(...)` then `forceBallot()`), which is the one deadline shape that *does* re-derive correctly. Design §Tests item 9 and 11 both describe this shape only.

## Could not determine

- **Whether F1 ever fires in the hosted fleet.** It needs a real episode whose play budget expires at or after the ballot turn; CI's offline episode is scripted and finishes in seconds (`reason=complete`). What would settle it: a phase-60 run whose `results.reason == "deadline"` — then compare that replay's `results.reason` against the `reason` field of the last frame the static bundle derives. (F1 itself is settled: reproduced by execution.)
- **The player container's blocking read has no explicit bound.** `tribunal_player.nim:62` calls `socket.receiveMessage()`, and whisky's signature is `receiveMessage*(ws: WebSocket, timeout = -1)` (`~/.nimby/pkgs/whisky/src/whisky.nim:73`) — `-1` blocks indefinitely. It terminates in practice because the game always reaches `quit(0)` (`server.nim:202`) from a loop that is itself bounded, closing the socket and raising into F7's handler; and the bullwhip starter does exactly the same (`bullwhip_player.nim:54`). I read checklist item 5's "no unbounded loop or blocking read" as scoped to the three waits it enumerates ("LLM call, seat reply, round barrier"), all of which are bounded here, so I have **not** filed this as blocking — but a stricter literal reading would, and the judge should rule. What would settle it: a decision on whether item 5 covers the player container, or a whisky call with an explicit timeout.
- **`episodeTimeoutSeconds` is validated only by the manifest schema.** `types.nim:84-122` (`update`) validates `rounds >= 2` and `players.len == 5` and nothing else; a config with `episodeTimeoutSeconds: 0` would make `playDeadline == 0.0` and disable the deadline guard entirely (`server.nim:249-253`). Confirmed by execution that `update` accepts it. Unreachable through the platform, which validates against `config_schema` (`minimum: 60`), and identical to bullwhip (`starters/cogame-bullwhip/src/bullwhip/types.nim:72, 103`). What would settle it: confirmation that the commissioner always validates `game_config` against `config_schema` before handing it to the container.
- **Turn-loop liveness if a seat can never act.** `server.nim:299-320` skips a seat whose decision *and* whose scripted fallback both raise, leaving `acted[seat] == false` and the same turn pending next iteration. I could not construct such a seat — `scriptedAction` is legal by construction (only names cards it holds; `scriptedArgument` is never empty, `llm.nim:183-205`) — so the branch reads as genuinely unreachable, and the pre-turn deadline check terminates the loop regardless. What would settle it: nothing short of a fault-injection test; recording it so the reliance on the deadline guard is explicit.
